## 04. `3.overview.tex`：收紧 Fine-Tuning 分类规则

中文说明：位置在 `Classification rules` 小节第一条规则。把“只要报告 fine-tuning 就归 Fine-Tuning”的高风险表述，改成“主要经验贡献和主要 repair result 依赖模型适配时才投影为 Fine-Tuning”。这直接降低 Reviewer 2 对 hybrid systems 的攻击面。

原文：

```latex
First, if the paper reports task-specific supervised fine-tuning, parameter-efficient fine-tuning, reinforcement-learning fine-tuning, or distillation, we assign the primary paradigm \emph{Fine-Tuning}, even if the adapted model is later wrapped inside a more complex procedure.
```

新内容：

```latex
First, we check whether the paper's main empirical contribution and repair results depend on adapting an LLM to repair data. We assign the primary paradigm \emph{Fine-Tuning} when task-specific supervised fine-tuning, parameter-efficient fine-tuning, reinforcement-learning fine-tuning, or distillation is the dominant repair mechanism. If an adapted model is only one component inside a procedural or agentic workflow, we still record the runtime control pattern and auxiliary evidence tags in the audit sheet rather than letting the adapted model alone determine the primary projection.
```

## 08. `response_letter.tex`：同步人工筛选 audit 说明

中文说明：位置在 Cover Letter corpus 更新段后。说明 raw agreement 是在裁决前计算，随后才裁决 7 个边界分歧，避免被理解成先裁决后算 kappa。

新增内容：

```latex
In addition to the released stage snapshots, we added a revision-stage human screening audit that can be checked directly from the artifact. Two external coders independently re-screened all 474 candidate records with the same inclusion/exclusion protocol. We also preserved a separate third raw screening pass and computed its agreement before any adjudication (467/474 records; Cohen's kappa $=0.94$). Only after computing this raw agreement did we adjudicate the seven boundary disagreements, and the resulting adjudicated labels preserve the final 66-system corpus used in the revised manuscript.
```
